Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your iPhone, in an encrypted database protected by Face ID or your device passcode. Mizan has no account and no server — your data is never uploaded to us.

How do I back up or move my data?

Go to Settings → Data to export an encrypted backup or a CSV/JSON file. Keep your backup passphrase safe — without it, an encrypted backup cannot be restored.

How do I erase everything?

Use Settings → Data → Erase all data, or delete the app from your iPhone.

How do budgets reset?

By default your budget follows the calendar month. In Settings → Budget you can set the reset day to your pay day, or tap “Reset budget now” to start a fresh cycle from today.

Is Mizan AI private?

Mizan AI is off by default. On supported iPhones it can run on-device (nothing is sent anywhere). Any server option is opt-in and only sends a small set of summary figures plus your question — never your individual transactions. See our Privacy Policy.
