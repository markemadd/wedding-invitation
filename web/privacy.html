Mizan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 July 2026

Data Not Collected · Everything stays on your device

Mizan is a private, on-device personal-finance app. It has no user accounts and no servers of its own. We — the developer — do not collect, receive, store, or have any access to your financial data. This policy explains how your information is handled.

The short version: Your money data lives only on your iPhone, in an encrypted database protected by Face ID / your device passcode. It is never uploaded to us, never sold, and never shared with advertisers or analytics companies.

Information Mizan stores on your device

Everything you enter or import — accounts, balances, transactions, budgets, goals, bills, and category settings — is stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database (AES encryption via SQLCipher), gated behind Face ID / Touch ID or your device passcode. This data does not leave your device as part of normal use.

Information we collect

None. Mizan contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind. We have no server that receives your data, so we cannot see it.

Camera, microphone, and photos

If you use receipt scanning or voice capture, the camera, photo library, microphone, and speech recognition are used only to create a transaction from what you capture, and the processing happens on your device. Mizan does not upload your photos or audio.

Optional AI features

Mizan AI is off by default. Depending on the option you choose:

  • Apple on-device intelligence (on supported iPhones): runs entirely on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Your own AI server (opt-in): if you turn this on and point Mizan at a computer you control, your typed question and a small set of summary figures (for example your monthly total, top categories, and net worth) are sent over your local network to your machine — not to us. Individual transactions are not sent.
  • Cloud AI with your own key (opt-in, where offered): the same small summary figures and your question are sent to the AI provider you configure, solely to answer your question. This only happens if you explicitly enable it.

Mizan AI explains your own numbers and is not financial advice.

Home Screen & Lock Screen widgets

Widgets read only a small, derived summary (such as your safe-to-spend amount) stored in a private on-device app group. Nothing is uploaded.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your data. There is no third party to share it with.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you are always in control. You can remove everything at any time by using Settings → Data → Erase all data, or by deleting the app. Any encrypted backup you export yourself is stored wherever you choose to save it.

Children

Mizan is not directed to children and is rated 4+. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the revised version at this URL.
